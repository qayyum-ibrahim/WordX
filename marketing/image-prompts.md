# WordX static ad images: ChatGPT prompts

AI image tools often spell text wrongly. Make the art with no text, then add the text in Canva or CapCut.

## Prompt 1: background art (9:16)
Vertical mobile game ad background, 9:16. Dark indigo-to-purple gradient with soft glowing orbs in violet, teal and orange. In the center, a glossy rounded game card showing five stacked clue bars, the top bar lit up and the other four dimmed. Five empty rounded letter tiles under the clues. Gold and violet confetti around the card. Clean modern flat-3D mobile UI style, playful, high contrast, lots of empty space at the top and bottom for text. No text, no letters, no logos, no people.

## Prompt 2: friends playing (4:5)
Vertical 4:5 social media ad illustration. Three young Nigerian friends laughing while looking at their phones together, bright expressive faces, warm lighting, bold purple and gold color palette, stylized illustration (not photorealistic). Floating rounded clue cards and sparkles around the phones. Space at the top for a headline. No text, no logos, no brand names.

## Prompt 3: letter tiles close-up (1:1)
Square 1:1 ad graphic. Big glossy 3D letter tiles with a gold tile in the middle, on a deep purple background with confetti and light rays. Playful, high energy, strong center focus. Leave the bottom third empty for text. No text on the tiles, no logos.

## Text to add
- Headline: "Guess the word from the clues"
- Line 2: "Fewer clues = more points"
- Button: "Play free"
- Small line: "Daily prize coming soon"

## Colors
Dark purple `#14102b`, violet `#7c4dff`, gold `#ffc928`.

## Rules
- Do not promise cash while the prize is paused.
- Check each platform's ad rules for prize promotions before paying for ads. The owner must also check Nigerian rules (not legal advice).
